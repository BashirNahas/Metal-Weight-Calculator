Set one was pure metal. Set two put the calculator back in. This set adds the missing third element — the balance — because the product is not a calculator and it is not a metal catalogue: it is a metal weight calculator. Same alloy palette, same tests: five sizes down to 24 px, three Android mask shapes, flattened to a single colour.

00 The brief الفكرة

Three ideas have to share one small square, so most of these routes make one shape do two jobs rather than crowding three symbols together. An equals sign that is also a balance. A calculator key with a scale cut through it. A beam whose pans are keys. Where a route does show all three separately, it earns it by keeping every element chunky enough to survive at 24 px.

01 The routes المقترحات

B-01يُساويThe Equals Fulcrum Cu 45Al 55

A copper bar, an aluminium wedge, an aluminium bar. Read it one way and it is an equals sign; read it the other and the top bar is a beam resting on a fulcrum above its plinth. One mark, two meanings, three shapes.

The best idea in any of the three sets. It says calculate and weigh simultaneously without drawing either literally, it is unbreakable at small sizes, and the double reading is the kind of thing people notice a week later and like the brand more for.

B-02الميزانُ الرقميThe Digital Balance Cu 40Al 60

A balance whose two pans are calculator keys — the copper one carrying the equals, and sinking, because copper is the denser metal.

The most literal fusion of all three ideas, and the tilt encodes real physics rather than decoration. Its cost is complexity: five distinct elements is the most of any route here, and 32 px is its honest floor.

B-04مفتاحُ الميزانThe Scale Key Cu 100

One solid copper calculator key with a balance milled clean through it — you see the background, not paint. The calculator is the object; the balance is the void inside it.

The most disciplined route: a single alloy, a single shape, and all the detail carried by negative space. Because the balance is a genuine hole, this mark keeps its full structure when flattened to one colour — it would work as a stamp, an embossing die or a laser cut without redrawing.

B-05القَبّانThe Steelyard Al 60Cu 40

The قَبّان — the single-arm steelyard hung from a ring, with the load on the short end and a sliding copper poise running along a graduated arm. The scale that has weighed metal in this region for a thousand years.

The most culturally rooted mark in any of the three sets, and the only one that would be recognised on sight by an older metal trader in Aleppo or Amman. It is also asymmetric, which makes it stand out sharply in a grid of centred app icons.

04 Recommendation التوصية

What I would ship from this set

B-01 «يُساوي» — the equals fulcrum. Across all twenty-seven marks in the three sheets, this is the one I would put my name on. Three shapes, two readings, zero fragility. It says calculate and weigh at once without illustrating either, and it will still look right in ten years.

B-04 «مفتاح الميزان» — the scale key — as the workhorse. A single copper key with the balance cut through it. One alloy, one silhouette, and the only mark here whose structure is fully intact in one colour, which makes it the right choice if the logo will ever be stamped, engraved or embroidered.

B-05 «القَبّان» — the steelyard — if you want to own the market. It is the scale your customers' fathers used to weigh copper, and no competing app looks remotely like it. The most distinctive route in the entire project, and the one with real emotional weight for an Arabic-speaking trade audience.

Compare across the sheets: pure metal · calculator × metal. All three share one palette, so a mark from one can be the app icon while a mark from another becomes the wordmark or feature-graphic device.

05 Taking one to production التنفيذ

  • Tell me the route code (B-01 … B-09, or any code from the other two sheets) and I will export clean SVG plus the full PNG set your build needs.
  • Icon source: replace assets/logo.png at 1024×1024, then run flutter pub run flutter_launcher_icons — pubspec.yaml is already wired for Android, iOS and web.
  • Adaptive icon: keep adaptive_icon_background: "#22223B" and supply the mark alone as the foreground at 66% of the canvas, so Android's circle mask never clips it.
  • Play Store: 512×512 icon (32-bit PNG, no alpha) plus a 1024×500 feature graphic.
  • Colour reference: copper #B87333 · copper highlight #E8A868 · aluminium #B4BCC5 · plate #0F0F1A → #22223B.
  • Version note: ship an icon change as 1.0.4+9 — version code 8 is already used on Play, so a rebuild at 8 will be rejected.
