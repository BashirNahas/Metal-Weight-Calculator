Every route is built from the same two alloys and tested the way an app icon actually gets used: at 24 px, under the three Android mask shapes, and flattened to a single colour. The name is the brief — نحاس means copper.

00 Why replace the current icon لماذا التغيير

The icon shipping today is a photoreal calculator crowded with gears, screws and coils. Three problems: the detail turns to mud below 64 px; a calculator body says arithmetic, not metal; and it carries no trace of the name that is the strongest asset the brand owns. Each route below fixes all three — and all nine share one alloy palette, so choosing any of them still gives you a coherent system.

The strongest letterform route: it carries the name, survives to 24 px because it is one bold silhouette, and the two-metal split reads even in monochrome as a change of value.

04 Recommendation التوصية

What I would ship

R-05 «النُّون» as the primary brand mark. It is the only route no competitor can copy, because it is built from your own name. It works on the splash screen, the film's logo-forge shot, letterheads and the website, and it reads as a considered abstract mark even to someone who cannot read Arabic.

R-06 «الطَّبَقات» as the Play Store icon if you want zero risk. Your store listing is worldwide in four languages; the stack says "metal, measured by thickness" with no decoding required, and it is the strongest of the nine at 24 px.

R-04 «المِيزان» as the dark horse. If the goal is to make someone understand the app before they read a word of the description, the tilted balance with two equal discs does more work than any other mark here.

All three share the identical copper and aluminium palette, so committing to one now does not lock out the others later — R-05 on the brand, R-06 on the store and R-04 in the onboarding illustration is a coherent system, not a compromise.
