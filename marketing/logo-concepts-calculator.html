Where the calculator
meets the metal

تسعةُ شعارات — حين تلتقي الآلةُ الحاسبة بالمعدن

The first sheet was pure metal. This one keeps the calculator in the mark — keys, operators, the readout, the equals sign — but casts every one of them in copper and aluminium. Same alloy palette, same tests: five sizes down to 24 px, three Android mask shapes, and flattened to a single colour.

00 The brief الفكرة

Your current icon already is a calculator — the problem was never the idea, it was the execution: photoreal clutter that turns to mud below 64 px. So this set keeps what the icon was reaching for and rebuilds it as flat geometry with real metal surfaces. Every route below still says "calculator" at a glance, but the material does the talking. Nothing here is decorative detail that dies when the icon shrinks.

01 The routes المقترحات

C-01المُساواةThe Equals Cu 50Al 50

The = sign built as two solid metal billets — a copper bar above an aluminium bar, each with a machined top face and end cut. The most recognisable glyph on any calculator, rendered as stock.

The strongest route in the set. It is two shapes, so it cannot break at any size; it reads as "equals" to everyone on earth; and "equals" is exactly what your app delivers — the answer. It also happens to be the only mark here that would work embossed on physical packaging.

C-03الشاشةThe Display Al 60Cu 40

An aluminium-bezelled readout with a copper round bar sitting where the digits would be, and the = waiting at the right. The screen is not showing a number — it is showing the metal itself.

The most conceptual route: it states the app's actual job, turning material into a figure. Two costs to weigh — the wide bezel fills a square icon less efficiently than the others, and because the readout is a dark inset rather than a cut-through, the mark flattens to a plain rounded rectangle in one colour. It needs a purpose-drawn outline version for stamps and embroidery.

C-05الجهازThe Device Al 55Cu 45

The whole calculator, rebuilt honestly: an aluminium body, a dark readout with a copper bar and an equals on it, and six chunky keys in alternating alloy. This is what your current icon was trying to be before the gears and screws buried it.

The safest evolution — existing users will recognise it as the same app. Three limits to accept: the portrait silhouette leaves side margins in a square icon, six keys is the ceiling before small sizes clog, and like C-03 it flattens to a plain rounded rectangle in one colour, so it needs a separate outline lockup for single-colour use.

A seven-segment digit with every segment milled from stock — the three horizontals in copper, the four verticals in aluminium. A number made of metal, which is the entire product in one image.

04 Recommendation التوصية

What I would ship from this set

C-07 «الرقم» — the segment digit. It is the one mark here that is simultaneously a calculator and a piece of metal, with no compromise on either half, and no competitor in the category looks like it. If you want the calculator idea kept but want to stop looking like every other calculator app, this is it.

C-01 «المُساواة» — the equals — if you want the safest possible icon. Two shapes, zero risk at any size, instantly understood in every language, and it carries the promise of the product: you get an answer.

C-05 «الجهاز» — the device — if continuity matters most. Existing testers already know a calculator icon; this is that same icon done properly. The least disruptive change you could make.

Compare against the pure-metal set in logo-concepts.html — both sheets share one palette, so a mark from either can be the app icon while a mark from the other becomes the wordmark or feature-graphic device.

05 Taking one to production التنفيذ

  • Tell me the route code (C-01 … C-09) and I will export clean SVG plus the full PNG set your build needs.
  • Icon source: replace assets/logo.png at 1024×1024, then run flutter pub run flutter_launcher_icons — pubspec.yaml is already wired for Android, iOS and web.
  • Adaptive icon: keep adaptive_icon_background: "#22223B" and supply the mark alone as the foreground at 66% of the canvas, so Android's circle mask never clips it.
  • Play Store: 512×512 icon (32-bit PNG, no alpha) plus a 1024×500 feature graphic.
  • Colour reference: copper #B87333 · copper highlight #E8A868 · aluminium #B4BCC5 · plate #0F0F1A → #22223B.
  • Version note: ship an icon change with a new version code — 1.0.4+9 — never by replacing a bundle already uploaded to Play.
